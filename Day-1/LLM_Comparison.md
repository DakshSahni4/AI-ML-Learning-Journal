1. **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** 

- Best Use Cases: Creative brainstorming, general-purpose text generation, web browsing, and multi-modal tasks (voice/image generation). 
- Strengths: Highly versatile, supports custom instructions/workflows, and boasts a massive ecosystem of Custom GPTs. 
- Limitations: Creative writing can sometimes feel "flat," and heavy usage can scale up costs. 
- When to choose it over others: Choose ChatGPT when you need an adaptable, "jack-of-all-trades" assistant that can browse the web, generate images, and hold voice conversations in one place.

2. **Claude (Anthropic)** 

- Best Use Cases: Long-document reading, legal/contract review, and high-quality, human-sounding content. 
- Strengths: Lowest error rate/hallucination risk when analyzing PDFs, and the most natural writing style that requires minimal editing. 
- Limitations: Fewer native multi-modal capabilities (less spontaneous with voice or image generation compared to ChatGPT/Gemini). 
- When to choose it over others: Choose Claude when accuracy, tone, and deep document analysis are your top priorities (e.g., summarizing a 100-page report or polishing a marketing email).
3. **Gemini (Google)** 

- Best Use Cases: Tasks integrated with Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Gmail) and complex multi-modal analysis (video, diagrams, audio). 
- Strengths: Native integration with Google apps and excellent cross-modal processing (translating a video into written summary). 
- Limitations: Creative and conversational outputs can occasionally feel stiff or heavily constrained. 
- When to choose it over others: Choose Gemini if your workflow lives inside Google Workspace or if you need to analyze large datasets and video files natively.

4. **DeepSeek** 
- Best Use Cases: Math problem solving, algorithm validation, and debugging code. 
- Strengths: Incredible cost-efficiency and remarkable technical reasoning/structural support. 
- Limitations: Less robust in conversational flow and lacks the creative writing prowess or massive plugin ecosystems of Western-developed models. 
- When to choose it over others: Choose DeepSeek if your primary goals involve highly technical coding or logic puzzles and you require a highly optimized, cost-effective tool.
